# 85 Proven Headline Templates

Fill-in-the-blank headlines proven across millions in sales. Use them as starting points — adapt to your specific brief, then test variants on real traffic.

---

## How to use this swipe file

1. **Pick a category** that matches your asset's job (curiosity for top-of-funnel, urgency for retargeting, etc.).
2. **Fill in the blanks** with content from your brief.
3. **Generate 5–10 variants** for the same offer using different templates.
4. **Test on real traffic.** Run two variants. Let the data pick the winner.

**Hard rules across all 85:**
- Under 12 words.
- Active voice.
- Specific over vague.
- No banned phrases (`voice-and-constraints.md`).
- Numbers > adjectives.

## Headline QA checklist

Before submitting any headline:

- [ ] Under 12 words
- [ ] Specific (number, name, timeframe, or concrete detail)
- [ ] Active voice
- [ ] No banned phrases (game-changer, unlock, revolutionary, etc.)
- [ ] No banned openers ("In today's world", "Are you tired of")
- [ ] Promises a specific benefit OR creates a curiosity gap
- [ ] Mobile-friendly length (60 characters max for emails)
- [ ] Honest — every claim is provable

---

## What to do when none of the templates fit

If 5 of these templates feel forced for your offer, the issue is usually the **brief**, not the templates. Specifically:

- **Audience isn't specific enough.** "Fitness coaches" is too broad. "Fitness coaches stuck at $5k/mo who hate posting on Instagram" lets you write 20 specific headlines.
- **Transformation is vague.** "More clients" is hard to headline. "5 booked calls per week without DM outreach" writes itself.
- **No proof point.** Without numbers or named clients, headlines stay abstract. Fix the brief, fix the headlines.

Go back to the brief, sharpen it, then return to this swipe file.
