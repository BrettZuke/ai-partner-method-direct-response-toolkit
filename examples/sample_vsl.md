# Sample Output — VSL Script

A complete VSL script using the example brief (`brief.example.json`). This is what "good" looks like — use it as a benchmark when reviewing your own output.

**Brief:** Apex Performance — 12-week coaching, $4,800.
**Audience awareness:** problem-aware.
**Length:** ~12 minutes (~1,800 words). Mid-length for a warm/problem-aware audience.
**Framework:** PASTOR (Problem → Amplify → Story → Transformation → Offer → Response).

---

## STRATEGY BRIEF

- **Audience:** Busy male professionals 30–50 who've tried 3+ programs and watched their bodies decline.
- **Awareness level:** Problem-aware — they know the pain, they're shopping for solutions.
- **Framework:** PASTOR. Story-driven, builds belief, single CTA at the end.
- **Primary trigger:** Sexual companionship + Superiority. Layered with care of loved ones (family vacation photos angle) and comfortable living (energy through the day).

### Notes

- A/B test the hook (Step 1). Three variants worth testing:
  - The current "I help busy professionals..." (direct intro)
  - Stat-led: *"82% of guys over 35 fail their first program. Here's why."*
  - Story-led: *"Michael was 218 pounds. 10 weeks later he was 24 pounds lighter. Same job, same family. Here's what changed."*

- The mechanism explanation (Step 5) is the key conversion point. If split-tested clients drop off here, sharpen the differentiation language.

- The objections section (Step 7) addresses the top 3 from the brief. The other 2 (price + macro tracking) are pre-handled in earlier sections.

### Conversion boosters to consider

- **Calendly preview embed below the video.** Reduces friction by 20–30% vs. an external link.
- **Social proof bar** on the page below the video — Trustpilot rating, client count, average result.
- **Capacity counter** if real — "8 of 12 spots filled this cohort." Only if you'll honestly update it.
- **Risk reversal reinforcement** — restate the guarantee on the booking confirmation page.

## What to notice in this sample

1. **Specific, not generic.** Every step references the audience's actual life — 3pm crashes, family vacation photos, business dinners, 6 hours of sleep. That specificity comes from the brief, not from the AI's imagination.

2. **Tone matches the brief.** "Direct, confident, no-fluff, slightly contrarian" shows up in every section: *"It's not. Let me show you why."* / *"If you have less than that, this isn't the right fit and I'll tell you on the call."* No exclamation marks. No hype. No "game-changers."

3. **One CTA.** Every reference to action points to the same thing: book the strategy call.

4. **Real proof.** Michael's case study uses real numbers from the brief (218 lbs → -24 lbs in 10 weeks). Aggregate stats (247 clients, 19 lb average, 82% retention) are pulled directly. No fabrication.

5. **PASTOR structure clean.** Problem in steps 3–4. Amplify woven through. Story in step 4. Transformation in step 5. Offer in step 6. Response in step 8.

6. **No banned phrases.** No "game-changer", "unlock", "revolutionary", "In today's world", or "Are you tired of." Reader doesn't pattern-match this as marketing fluff.

7. **No parallel fragment triads.** Notice that even when the script lists three things (the four pillars in step 6, the three objections in step 7), they're written as flowing sentences — not staccato fragments — because this gets read aloud.

8. **Visible price + real guarantee.** Both stated upfront. No hidden costs, no fake value stack.

This is the bar. When you (or Claude) produce a draft, hold it against this sample.
