# Long-Form Sales Page Template

**Goal:** Convert visitors into buyers with a story-driven, proof-heavy long-form page.

**Recommended framework:** PASTOR for cold/unaware traffic. PAS for warm/problem-aware. BAB for solution-aware.

**Target length by awareness level:**

| Audience awareness   | Word count       |
|----------------------|------------------|
| Unaware              | 4,000–6,000      |
| Problem-aware        | 3,000–4,000      |
| Solution-aware       | 2,000–3,000      |
| Product-aware        | 1,000–2,000      |
| Most-aware           | Under 1,000      |

Replace every `[bracketed placeholder]` with content from your brief.

## Section 2 — The Problem

**Goal:** Make the reader feel seen. Use their exact language.

```
[H2 subhead — names the problem in their words. E.g. "Why Most Coaches Hit a $5k/mo Ceiling and Stay There."]

[Paragraph 1 — describe the situation in detail. The reader should think "this is me." Use sensory language: what their day looks like, what they're tired of, what they tell themselves at 11pm on a Sunday.]

[Paragraph 2 — what they've already tried that didn't work. Validate the effort. Don't blame them. The point is "the system you're in is broken, not you."]

[Paragraph 3 — the cost of staying stuck. Money. Time. Relationships. Self-image. Stack the consequences.]
```

---

## Section 3 — The Agitation / Amplify

**Goal:** Make the cost of inaction unbearable.

```
[H2 subhead — frames the urgency. "Here's What 6 More Months Like This Looks Like."]

[Paragraph 1 — paint the future if nothing changes. 6 months from now. 1 year. 5 years. Be specific.]

[Paragraph 2 — the opportunity cost. While they're stuck, peers are doubling. Markets are shifting. Time is the asset they can't get back.]

[Paragraph 3 — the emotional cost. Confidence. Identity. Family impact. Self-respect.]

[Optional pull-quote in larger type — single sentence that crystallizes the cost. "Every month you wait is another month watching peers double up while you grind on the same broken funnel."]
```

## Section 4 — The Story / Mechanism

**Goal:** Introduce the unique mechanism through a story. Build belief.

```
[H2 subhead — story-style. "Here's What Changed Everything for [Founder/Client Name]."]

[Paragraph 1 — set the scene. Founder's situation OR a client's situation that mirrors the reader's.]

[Paragraph 2 — the turning point. What they discovered, who they met, what insight broke the loop.]

[Paragraph 3 — the mechanism. Name it. "The 3-Step [Methodology] System." Make it feel proprietary, but explainable in plain language.]

[Paragraph 5 — replication. They've now used this with X clients in Y industries. Specific.]

[Optional: founder photo + 1-line caption — "Hi, I'm [Name]. I help [audience] [transformation]."]
```

## Page-level rules

### CTAs
- **Single destination across the entire page.** Every button goes to the same offer.
- **Minimum 3 CTA buttons** through the page (after Hero, after Offer, after FAQ).
- Action-oriented text: "Book My Call" / "Get Instant Access" — never "Submit" or "Click Here."

### Visual rhythm
- Use whitespace generously between sections.
- Break up dense text with images, pull-quotes, and dividers.
- Mobile-test every section. Long paragraphs that look fine on desktop are unreadable on mobile.

### Reading level
- 6th grade. Hemingway Editor or similar.
- Anything above 8th grade = bounce on mobile.

### Proof density
- At least 1 specific number every 200–300 words.
- At least 1 named testimonial in the first half of the page.
- Stat callouts (large-type numbers) every 1–2 sections in long-form pages.

## Customization checklist

Before publishing:

- [ ] Headline filled from `swipe-headlines.md` and tested against alternatives
- [ ] All `[brackets]` replaced with brief content (search for "[" before publish)
- [ ] CTAs go to the *same* destination throughout
- [ ] Pricing visible and accurate
- [ ] Guarantee terms match what you can actually deliver
- [ ] Testimonials are real (full name, real photo, accurate quote)
- [ ] No banned phrases (`voice-and-constraints.md`)
- [ ] Active voice throughout
- [ ] Mobile-tested at 375px (iPhone SE), 390px (iPhone 14), 768px (iPad)
- [ ] Read out loud — sounds like the client, not generic AI
- [ ] Page load time under 3 seconds
- [ ] At least 3 CTA buttons placed throughout
- [ ] FAQ addresses the top 5–7 objections from the brief

## Common mistakes

| Mistake                                              | Fix                                                       |
|------------------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------|
| Hero headline too clever, too vague                  | Pull from `swipe-headlines.md`. Specific over clever.     |
| Multiple offers / multiple CTAs                       | One offer per page. Always.                                |
| Pricing buried at the bottom                         | Visible by section 8 at latest. Hidden price = high bounce. |
| Generic testimonials ("Great program! Loved it.")    | Specific results with named clients only.                  |
| 8-paragraph Problem section                          | Max 3 paragraphs per section. Use subheads to break.       |
| FAQ that doesn't address real objections             | Pull from brief's `objections` field. The top 5 actual ones. |
| No mobile preview before publish                     | 60%+ traffic is mobile. Always test.                       |
| Fabricated value stack ($1,997 value!!!)             | Use real component prices or skip the calculation entirely. |
