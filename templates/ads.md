# Paid Ad Scripts

**Goal:** Hook attention in 3 seconds, communicate the offer in under 60 seconds, drive a single click.

**Platforms covered:**
- Facebook / Instagram (feed + reels)
- YouTube (in-stream + bumper)
- TikTok (organic-style paid)

---

## Ad-specific rules

These differ from long-form copy:

- **Hook in 3 seconds** — text on screen, opening line, or visual pattern interrupt. If they don't pause/lean in by 3 seconds, you've lost them.
- **One core idea per ad** — don't cram three benefits into 30 seconds.
- **Visual + audio together** — always. Most viewers watch on mute first.
- **Subtitles always** — for the muted-watching majority.
- **CTA in the last 5 seconds** — and on the destination page they land on.

## Template 1 — Direct Response Hook → Offer (Best for FB/IG cold)

**Length:** 30–60 seconds
**Use when:** introducing a new audience to a clear offer

```
[0–3 SECONDS — HOOK]
[On screen: bold text claim or stat]
[Voiceover: contrarian claim or specific stat]

Examples:
- "73% of fitness coaches make under $4k a month."
- "I lost 24 lbs in 10 weeks without touching cardio."
- "Most people use [common method]. Here's why it doesn't work."

[3–10 SECONDS — IDENTIFY AUDIENCE]
"If you're [specific audience description], you need to hear this."

Examples:
- "If you're a coach stuck under $5k/month..."
- "If you're a man over 40 watching your body change..."
- "If you've tried 3+ programs and none of them stuck..."

[10–30 SECONDS — DELIVER THE INSIGHT / PROMISE]
[1–2 sentences: the specific insight or transformation. Be concrete.]

Examples:
- "The reason you're stuck isn't your offer. It's the 70% of leads who don't buy on day one — the people you're never following up with."
- "Most workout programs fail because they ignore the one thing that determines results past 35: recovery quality."

[30–50 SECONDS — PROOF + OFFER]
"I've helped [specific number] [audience] [specific outcome]. Here's what we do:
[3-bullet list — what's included, what they get]
[Price + format if relevant]"

[50–60 SECONDS — CTA]
[On screen: button or text]
"Click below to [specific action]. Spots are limited."

[Subtitle on screen for the full duration]
```

## Template 2 — Story / UGC Style (Best for FB/IG retargeting + warm audiences)

**Length:** 60–90 seconds
**Use when:** the audience already knows you exist or has engaged with your brand before

```
[0–5 SECONDS — RELATABLE OPENING]
[Founder or client speaking direct to camera, casual setting]
"6 months ago I was [specific bad situation]. Now I'm [specific good situation]. Here's what changed."

[5–20 SECONDS — THE BEFORE STATE]
"I was doing [common-but-wrong approach]. I tried [thing 1], [thing 2], [thing 3]. Nothing worked."

[20–40 SECONDS — THE TURNING POINT]
"Then I discovered [insight or system]. Here's the simple version: [explain in 2–3 sentences]."

[40–55 SECONDS — THE RESULT]
"Within [timeframe], I [specific result]. [Optional: specific number]."

[55–80 SECONDS — INVITATION]
"If you want me to walk you through how this works, click below. I put together a [free training / call / guide] that goes deeper."

[80–90 SECONDS — CTA]
[On screen: button]
"Tap [button text] to [action]. Takes 30 seconds."
```

## Template 3 — Educational / Authority (Best for YouTube + cold)

**Length:** 60–120 seconds
**Use when:** building authority before any sales ask. Higher cost-per-lead but better-quality leads.

```
[0–5 SECONDS — CURIOSITY HOOK]
"Most [audience] do [common thing]. Here's why that's the wrong approach."

[5–25 SECONDS — VALIDATE THE PROBLEM]
"You've probably been told [common advice]. The reason that doesn't work is [contrarian insight + brief explanation]."

[25–60 SECONDS — DELIVER REAL VALUE]
[Actually teach something useful. The viewer should learn something even if they never click.]

"Here's what actually works: [step 1]. Then [step 2]. Then [step 3]. The reason this works is [mechanism]."

[60–90 SECONDS — BRIDGE TO OFFER]
"That's the high-level version. If you want the full breakdown — including [specific bonus content] — I put together a [free training / guide / call]."

[90–120 SECONDS — CTA]
"Click below to access it. It's free, no opt-in tricks."
[On screen: button]
```

## Template 4 — Bumper / Short-Form (TikTok, IG Reels, YouTube Shorts)

**Length:** 6–15 seconds
**Use when:** the platform demands brevity. Goal is recognition + click, not full conversion.

```
[0–2 SECONDS — VISUAL HOOK]
[On screen: bold claim, surprising visual, or pattern interrupt]

[2–6 SECONDS — ONE-LINE PROMISE]
[Voiceover or on-screen text: single sentence that delivers the entire pitch]

Examples:
- "I made my first $10k freelance month using only 3 LinkedIn templates."
- "This 12-minute morning routine fixed my energy crashes."

[6–10 SECONDS — PROOF OR EXPANSION]
[One specific detail that makes the claim credible]

Examples:
- "Sent 47 cold messages. Booked 8 calls. Closed 3."
- "No coffee. No supplements. Just these 3 things in this order."

[10–15 SECONDS — CTA]
[On screen: link in bio / swipe up / link below]
"Get the full breakdown — link [in bio / below]."
```

## Template 5 — Social Proof / Case Study (Best for retargeting)

**Length:** 30–60 seconds
**Use when:** the audience knows you and needs the final push

```
[0–3 SECONDS — CLIENT NAME + RESULT]
[Text on screen: "Sarah K — 0 clients to 12 in 90 days"]
[Voiceover: "Meet Sarah."]

[3–15 SECONDS — BEFORE STATE]
"Sarah was running a yoga studio that had been losing money for 18 months. Three months from closing."

[15–35 SECONDS — TRANSFORMATION]
"Today: 12 monthly members. 80% capacity. Hiring her first instructor. Net revenue $11k a month."

[35–50 SECONDS — THE MECHANISM]
"What changed? She built a referral system that turns members into recruiters. Took her one weekend."

[50–60 SECONDS — CTA]
"Want to see how it works for your business? Link below."
```

## Hook bank (the first 3 seconds — pick from these)

The single biggest determinant of ad performance is the hook. Test 5+ variants per ad.

### Stat hooks
- "73% of [audience] never reach [outcome]."
- "[Specific number] [audience] are stuck at [pain point]."
- "Less than 10% of [audience] are doing [thing] correctly."

### Contrarian hooks
- "Everything you've been told about [topic] is wrong."
- "Stop [doing common thing]. Do this instead."
- "The reason [common goal] feels hard is [counterintuitive answer]."

### Question hooks
- "Why do [audience] never reach [outcome]?"
- "What if [common belief] is actually backwards?"
- "Why is [thing they want] so hard for [audience]?"

### Story hooks
- "6 months ago I was [bad situation]. Now [good situation]. Here's what changed."
- "Last year, [Client] was [bad state]. Today, [good state]."
- "I lost $X / spent X years / made X mistakes before figuring this out."

### Pattern interrupt hooks
- [Visual jump-cut to a result or outcome — no preamble]
- [Hands holding a specific object that represents the offer]
- [Numbers on screen counting up to the result]

## Customization checklist (every ad)

- [ ] Hook in 3 seconds (text or visual)
- [ ] One core idea — not three
- [ ] Subtitles burned in (most viewers watch muted)
- [ ] CTA in last 5 seconds
- [ ] CTA destination matches (don't promise "free training" and link to a sales page)
- [ ] Visual matches the platform (vertical for IG/TikTok/Reels, square or 16:9 for FB feed and YouTube)
- [ ] Length matches the platform (15–60s for FB/IG, 30–120s for YouTube, 6–15s for Shorts/Reels/TikTok)
- [ ] Test 3–5 hook variants
- [ ] Read out loud — sounds like a real person, not a marketer
- [ ] No banned phrases (`voice-and-constraints.md`)

## Common ad mistakes

| Mistake                                              | Fix                                                            |
|------------------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------|
| 8-second logo intro                                   | Cut. Hook in 3 seconds means 3 seconds of *value*, not branding. |
| No subtitles                                         | 80%+ of social ads watched muted. Subtitles = non-negotiable.   |
| Generic stock footage                                | Real founder/client footage outperforms stock by 30–50%.        |
| 3 CTAs at the end                                    | One.                                                            |
| Promising X, linking to Y                            | The destination must match the promise. Mismatch = no conversion. |
| Same hook across all platforms                       | Test different hooks per platform. What works on TikTok may flop on YouTube. |
| 60-second ad with one boring visual the whole time   | Cut every 2–4 seconds. Visual rhythm matters more than copy beauty. |
| Ad that sells immediately on cold traffic            | Cold traffic needs education first. Use Template 3.             |
| "Click the link in bio" without telling them why     | Always reinforce the *value* of clicking. "Get the full breakdown" not just "click below." |
